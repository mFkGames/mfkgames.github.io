Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

At mFk Games (accessible from https://mfkgames.github.io/), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and players. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by mFk Games and how we use it across our website, games, and mobile applications.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at mfkgames.support@gmail.com.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your full name, physical address, or phone number) directly on our website unless you choose to contact us. However, our mobile applications and games distributed through official stores (Google Play Store and Apple App Store) may utilize external services and SDKs that collect information used to provide core features, gameplay analytics, and advertisements.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

Our mobile games and apps integrate trusted third-party libraries and SDKs to support game functionality, secure payments, crash diagnostics, and advertising. These third parties access non-personal data (such as device model, OS version, advertising identifiers, and anonymous usage statistics) in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Apple App Store & Apple Privacy
  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Crashlytics

3. Apple iOS Guidelines & App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

For users on iOS devices, our applications comply strictly with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If an application utilizes advertising or tracking technologies, it requests your explicit permission before collecting device identifiers (IDFA). You may adjust or revoke tracking preferences at any time through your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

4. Log Data and Crash Diagnostics

In the event of an error, bug, or crash while playing our games, diagnostic Log Data is collected automatically via standard developer tools (such as Firebase Crashlytics). This data may include device name, operating system version, app state at the time of the event, and basic performance metrics. This information is utilized solely to fix crashes and enhance game stability.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting children using mobile devices and the internet is a fundamental commitment for us. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Where games are directed to families or children, we strictly adhere to COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) guidelines and GDPR-K provisions, avoiding behavioral profiling or personalized ad tracking. If you believe your child has provided personal information to us, please reach out immediately and we will promptly remove the data.

6. Data Security

We value your trust and implement commercially acceptable physical and electronic practices to safeguard any data transmitted to or stored by our services. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely infallible.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our games, legal requirements, or store policies. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data rights, please contact us directly:

  • Email: mfkgames.support@gmail.com
  • Website: https://mfkgames.github.io/
